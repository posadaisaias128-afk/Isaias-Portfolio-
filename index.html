About Me

I'm an undergraduate student at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), pursuing a degree in Aeronautics and Astronautics (Course 16). My academic focus and passion lie in aircraft propulsion — from jet engine design and thermodynamic cycle analysis to next-generation sustainable aviation technologies.

I'm driven by the challenge of designing efficient, reliable propulsion systems that push the boundaries of aerospace engineering. Through coursework, hands-on projects, and CAD modeling, I continue to develop the technical skills needed to contribute to the future of flight.
